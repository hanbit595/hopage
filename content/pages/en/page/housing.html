Residential Support Program

If renewable energy systems such as solar PV, solar thermal, geothermal, small wind power, and fuel cells are installed in residential homes, the government provides partial financial support for installation costs.
This program reduces fossil fuel consumption while simultaneously lowering electricity bills and carbon emissions.

Eligible Housing Types
  • Detached House
    Owners of existing or newly built houses
  • Existing Apartment Housing
    Apartment owners or residents’ representative bodies
  • New Apartment Housing
    Developers, constructors, or residents’ representative bodies

※ Buildings owned by the national or local governments are not eligible

※ Solar PV installation is only available when the KEPCO contract type is residential

What Is a Solar-Powered Home?

An eco-friendly residential house where electricity generated by installing solar modules on the roof, rooftop, or windows is directly consumed within the household.

  • Typical installation capacity per household: 3kW
  • Required installation area: Approx. 23㎡

Process
  • 01
    Sign Up & Select Participating Company
    The applicant signs up on the NR website and selects the region, energy source, and participating company.
    Applicant
  • 02
    Contract Review & Execution
    The participating company reviews the application and proceeds with the contract.
    Participating Company
  • 03
    Submission of Project Application
    After signing the contract, the applicant submits the project application to the Renewable Energy Center.
    Applicant
  • 04
    Document Review & Approval
    The center reviews the submitted documents and grants approval. (SMS notification sent)
    Renewable Energy Center
  • 05
    Self-Funding Notice & Payment
    After receiving virtual account details, the applicant must pay the self-funded amount within 7 days.
    Applicant
  • 06
    Installation Commencement & Completion
    The participating company carries out the installation, and the center verifies the results.
    Participating Company
  • 07
    Subsidy Payment
    The center disburses the government subsidy and the applicant’s
    contribution to the participating company    .
    Renewable Energy Center
