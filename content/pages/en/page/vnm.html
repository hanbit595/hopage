VNM

A virtual net-metering program that enables anyone to benefit from solar energy,
providing access to solar energy benefits without installing solar panels on their own rooftop.

NEM (Net Energy Metering)

Net Metering System
  • 01
    Solar Power Generation
    Electricity generated through rooftop solar panels

  • 02
    On-site Priority Consumption
    Generated electricity is immediately consumed on-site

  • 03
    Surplus Power Generation
    Excess electricity after on-site usage

  • 04
    Transmission to KEPCO
    Surplus power transmitted to the grid

  • 05
    Electricity Bill Deduction
    Electricity charges offset by transmitted amount
Used within the same building, and surplus electricity is directly offset.

VNM (Virtual Net Metering)

Virtual Net Metering System
  • 01
    Remote Solar Power Generation
    Electricity generated at a solar power plant
    located in another area

  • 02
    Grid Connection
    Generated power transmitted
    to the KEPCO grid

  • 03
    Power Metering (kWh)
    Measurement of generated electricity

  • 04
    Conversion to Credit
    kWh converted into billing credits

  • 05
    Credit Accumulation
    Credits stored in the subscriber’s account

  • 06
    Electricity Bill Deduction
    Credits applied to reduce bills
    of another building
Solar power is generated remotely, and the bill is reduced here.
Key Features
Flexible Transaction Model
Supports various scenarios such as bill deduction, progressive distribution, revenue settlement, and DR integration
Multi-Site Integration
Integrated management of distributed generation and consumption sites including municipalities, educational institutions, public and private sectors
Automation & Real-Time Management
Visualizes generation, consumption, and revenue data while automating settlement processes
Differentiation Points
Optimization of shared solar and distributed resources, applicable to diverse participants
Virtual Net Metering (VNM) Operating Structure
  • Invest in a desired capacity of a solar power plant and deduct the generated electricity from your consumption
  • Various investment models available
  • Execution of various VNM-related operations such as settlement, customer acquisition, and data management
  • Operable by utility companies
  • Operable under special purpose entities (SPE)
