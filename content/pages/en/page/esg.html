Designing a Sustainable Tomorrow

HANBIT Innotech practices sustainable energy management
where Environment (E), Social (S), and Governance (G) are in balance,
grounded in technology, responsibility, and trust.

HANBIT’S ENVIRONMENT · SOCIAL · GOVERNANCE
  • Environment
    Achieving Carbon Neutrality through
    BIPV · ESS · Recycling Technologies
  • Social
    Creating Shared Value through
    Public Projects · Education ·
    Social Contribution
  • Governance
    Establishing an Ethical, Fair,
    ESG-Based Management System
A Sustainable Future Powered by Eco-friendly Energy
  • -Building Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV) and Energy Storage System (ESS)
  • -Recycling of end-of-life solar panels and batteries
  • -Creating a resource-circulating energy ecosystem
Energy Transition Together with the Community
  • -Public sector energy welfare projects
  • -Solar energy education programs
  • -Ongoing community contribution initiatives
Establishing a Governance Framework built on Ethics, Fairness, and ESG Principles
  • -Compliance with regulations and ethical management
  • -Structured and fair decision-making process
  • -Strengthening social responsibility through ESG management
Realization of ESG Management
  • -Simultaneously strengthens eco-friendly brand image and fulfills social responsibility
  • -High-efficiency solar power technology development
  • -Optimization of energy production, storage, and utilization through smart technologies
