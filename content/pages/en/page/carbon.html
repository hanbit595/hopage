BIPV-Based Carbon Credits

Solar power generation reduces electricity costs
while creating carbon credit revenue.
Based on BIPV operational data, carbon reduction performance is calculated and linked to carbon credit issuance and trading.

Carbon Credit Process

01
BIPV Installation
Installation of building-integrated photovoltaic (BIPV) systems on façades and rooftops
  • Integrated design of structural and electrical systems
  • Construction, quality control, and completion inspection
02
Solar Power Generation
Eco-friendly electricity generation from installed BIPV systems
  • Self-consumption + surplus power generation
  • Electricity cost savings
03
Generation Data Collection
Real-time measurement and automatic storage of power generation data
  • Monitoring of generation output and operational status
  • Securing baseline data for emission reduction calculation
04
Emission Reduction Calculation
Automatic calculation of greenhouse gas reduction based on generation data
  • Conversion of power output into carbon reduction volume
  • Application of external reduction project standards
05
Reduction Certification
Third-party verification and official certification procedures
  • Response to verification agencies
  • Document submission and approval support
06
Carbon Credit Issuance
Conversion of certified reduction performance into carbon credits
  • Issuance of credits per company
  • Account registration and asset management
07
Carbon Credit Trading & Monetization
Revenue generation through market trading
  • Carbon credit sales
  • Securing cash revenue or operating a holding strategy
One-Stop Consulting for External Reduction Projects
  • Customized solutions based on PV and BIPV
  • Full support from certification → approval → trading
  • Regulatory compliance + revenue generation + ESG enhancement
