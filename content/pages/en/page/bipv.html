BIM Design
By precisely reviewing power generation simulation and structural stability from the installation stage,
we establish a design foundation capable of stable operation for decades.
  • BIM-linked Simulation
    Automatically analyzes solar radiation, shadow impact, and building envelope conditions
    to secure optimal energy performance.
  • BIPV-BIM Integrated Analysis
    Quantitatively reviews generation efficiency, economic feasibility, and installation suitability
    through BIM-based evaluation specialized for BIPV design.
  • Design–Construction Data Integration
    Provides analysis results as data applicable to the construction stage
    to maximize on-site implementation efficiency.
