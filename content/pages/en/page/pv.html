01
Building-Type Solar Power
A model that enables efficient energy production by installing solar systems on rooftops or exterior walls of existing buildings without requiring additional land.
  • Self-Consumption Model
    This model focuses on reducing utility costs by consuming the electricity generated on-site. Widely used in factories, logistics centers, and commercial facilities. Any surplus power can be sold back to the grid (KEPCO).
  • Public Support Project Model
    This model reduces the burden of initial installation costs through government or municipal subsidies while supporting eco-friendly policies. Ideal for achieving energy independence in public buildings, schools, and welfare facilities.
  • Lease & Private Investment Model
    A profit-sharing model where the building owner leases the rooftop or site, and private investors handle installation and operations. Generates stable income through rent or generation profits without the burden of initial investment costs.
02
Agrivoltaics
A model that maximizes land-use value by combining agriculture with solar power, simultaneously realizing crop production and energy generation.
  • Dual-Use Farming Model
    Solar structures are installed above crop cultivation areas to allow for simultaneous farming and power generation. This creates additional revenue streams while maintaining the original productivity of the farmland.
  • Cooperatives & Corporate Model
    Farmers form cooperatives or corporations to jointly invest and operate the system. By sharing profits with members and the local community, this model promotes economic self-reliance in rural areas.
  • Lease / In-Kind Trading Model
    A model that secures stable income by leasing farmland or trading generated electricity in kind. It is gaining attention as a win-win model between landowners and power generation operators.
03
Floating Solar Power
An eco-friendly power generation method installed on idle water surfaces, ensuring high generation efficiency without land damage.
  • Public Reservoir & Dam Model
    Installed on agricultural reservoirs or dam surfaces, enabling large-scale power generation. This model allows stable project operation led by public institutions.
  • Private Consortium Model
    Private companies form consortia to jointly invest and operate the system, combining technical expertise and capital to efficiently implement projects.
  • Community Return Model
    A win-win model that returns a portion of power generation profits to local residents and municipalities, building a sustainable energy ecosystem together with the community.
