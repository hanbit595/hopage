Building Support Program

This program provides government subsidies for a portion of installation costs
when renewable energy systems (PV · BIPV) are installed in general buildings excluding residential houses.

  • Support is provided only for self-consumption installations
  • Electricity sales are not permitted
  • Supported capacity may be adjusted based on committee evaluation
  • Buildings subject to mandatory installation by local governments are excluded
  • Reduces electricity costs and enhances ESG · RE100 response and corporate image
Eligible Buildings
  • General Buildings
    Owners of commercial buildings such as retail stores, factories, warehouses, and churches
  • Corporate / Industrial Complex Buildings
    Companies pursuing RE100 and tenants within industrial complexes
  • Special-Purpose Facilities
    Business facilities such as livestock barns and agricultural buildings

※ Support is limited to self-consumption purposes only

※ Buildings subject to mandatory local government installation and public buildings are excluded

Support Details

As of 2025, installation subsidies are provided for Photovoltaic (PV) and Building-Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) systems.

Process
  • 01
    Application for Project Participation
    Participating company applies for the project to the Renewable Energy Center
    Participating Company
  • 02
    Contract Review and Execution
    The Center reviews company eligibility and selects the participant
    Renewable Energy Center
  • 03
    Application by Installation Applicant
    Building owner applies after signing a contract with the participating company
    Building Owner
  • 04
    Application Review and Approval
    The Center reviews submitted documents and grants approval
    Renewable Energy Center
  • 05
    Facility Inspection and Subsidy Payment
    After installation completion → on-site inspection and confirmation →
    subsidy paid by the Center to the participating company
    Renewable Energy Center
