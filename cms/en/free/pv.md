                        <div class="num">01</div>
                        <div class="tit">Building-Type Solar Power</div>
                        <div class="txt">A model that enables efficient energy production by installing solar systems on
                            rooftops or exterior walls of existing buildings without requiring additional land.</div>
                    </div>
                    <div class="buis_img">
                        <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_01.jpg" alt="">
                    </div>
                    <ul class="buis_detail_list">
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_01_01.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Self-Consumption Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> This model focuses on reducing utility costs by consuming the
                                    electricity generated on-site. Widely used in factories, logistics centers, and
                                    commercial facilities. Any surplus power can be sold back to the grid (KEPCO).
                                </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_01_02.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Public Support Project Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> This model reduces the burden of initial installation costs through
                                    government or municipal subsidies while supporting eco-friendly policies. Ideal for
                                    achieving energy independence in public buildings, schools, and welfare facilities.
                                </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_01_03.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Lease & Private Investment Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> A profit-sharing model where the building owner leases the rooftop or
                                    site, and private investors handle installation and operations. Generates stable
                                    income through rent or generation profits without the burden of initial investment
                                    costs. </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                    </ul>
                </section>
                <section class="buis_section buis_section_02">
                    <div class="go_target" id="pv2"></div>
                    <div class="buis_tit">
                        <div class="num">02</div>
                        <div class="tit">Agrivoltaics</div>
                        <div class="txt">A model that maximizes land-use value by combining agriculture with solar
                            power, simultaneously realizing crop production and energy generation.</div>
                    </div>
                    <div class="buis_img">
                        <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_02.jpg" alt="">
                    </div>
                    <ul class="buis_detail_list">
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_02_01.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Dual-Use Farming Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> Solar structures are installed above crop cultivation areas to allow
                                    for simultaneous farming and power generation. This creates additional revenue
                                    streams while maintaining the original productivity of the farmland. </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_02_02.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Cooperatives & Corporate Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> Farmers form cooperatives or corporations to jointly invest and
                                    operate the system. By sharing profits with members and the local community, this
                                    model promotes economic self-reliance in rural areas. </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                        <li class="buis_detail_card">
                            <div class="ico">
                                <img src="/theme/basic/images/sub/pv_ico_02_03.png" alt="">
                            </div>
                            <div class="detail_info_wrap">
                                <div class="tit">Lease / In-Kind Trading Model</div>
                                <div class="txt"> A model that secures stable income by leasing farmland or trading
                                    generated electricity in kind. It is gaining attention as a win-win model between
                                    landowners and power generation operators. </div>
                            </div>
                        </li>
                    </ul>
                </section>
