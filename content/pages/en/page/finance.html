Financial Support Program

A long-term, low-interest loan support program for the installation of renewable energy facilities and equipment for the production of dedicated products.

Funding Support Conditions

Category Details
Facility Funds Purchase cost of applicable facilities (excluding used equipment) and auxiliary equipment, installation and renovation costs (including grid connection costs), repair, design and supervision costs (including technology introduction costs), test operation expenses, etc. (However, hydro facilities exceeding 5MW are excluded)

- VAT, land purchase costs, construction expenses not directly related to building installation, and licensing costs are excluded

Eligible Facilities

Category Details
Solar Thermal Facilities Facilities for producing and installing solar thermal collectors certified under renewable energy equipment certification, KS-certified products, or equivalent equipment (excluding natural circulation integrated solar thermal systems)
Solar Power
Facilities		 Facilities for power generation and transmission, or facilities for producing such equipment
Including resident participation portions such as bonds, stocks, and funds of village enterprises participating in power generation projects
Process
  • 01
    Project Announcement
    The New & Renewable Energy Center announces the project and confirms
    eligible applicants after document review.
    New & Renewable Energy Center
  • 02
    Online Application for Funding
    Applicants submit their funding application online.
    Applicant
  • 03
    Document Review and Evaluation
    The Center/Comprehensive Support Center reviews the submitted documents,
    requests supplementation if necessary, and conducts the final evaluation.
    Center/Comprehensive Support Center
  • 04
    Issuance of Recommendation Letter
    After evaluation is completed, the Center issues a recommendation letter.
    Center/Comprehensive Support Center
  • 05
    Loan Application
    After receiving the recommendation letter, the applicant visits a financial institution (bank)
    for loan screening and approval.
    Applicant
  • 06
    Fund Disbursement (Initial and Interim Withdrawals)
    After loan approval, the initial withdrawal is made,
    and additional withdrawals may be made until the end of the year if necessary.
    Applicant
  • 07
    Facility Installation and Completion Inspection
    Once installation is completed, inspection and completion verification are conducted.
    After certification procedures are completed, the remaining funds are disbursed.
    Applicant
  • 08
    Post-Management
    After final completion, on-site inspections and operational management
    procedures are carried out.
    Center/Comprehensive Support Center
