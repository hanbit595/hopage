Distributed Energy

“Power, produced closer and more efficiently.”
As a next-generation energy system that moves beyond centralized power generation
to direct production and consumption near demand sites,
it is a key solution for achieving carbon neutrality, RE100, and energy self-sufficiency.

  • Rooftop Solar Power in Industrial Complexes
    Utilizing idle spaces, reducing electricity costs, and securing a foundation for RE100 implementation,
    we support sustainable energy transition through industrial complex-tailored design and construction.
  • ESS Farm
    By storing energy to mitigate peak demand, reduce costs,
    and participate in the power trading market, industrial-scale ESS Farms
    enable stable power operation and energy self-sufficiency.
  • Integrated Energy Platform
    Real-time monitoring of solar, ESS, and consumption data,
    tracking RE100, carbon management, and power trading,
    connecting all energy systems into one unified platform.
