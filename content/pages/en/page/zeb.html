ZEB

Zero Energy Building (ZEB) is a future-oriented building model that minimizes
the energy load required for building operation and achieves energy self-sufficiency
through the use of renewable energy.

※ Mandatory Grade 4 requirement for public buildings (17 building types) with a total floor area of 1,000㎡ or more

Applicable energy sources for buildings include solar PV, solar thermal, geothermal, and fuel cells.
Among these, the most practical and immediately deployable solution is solar power generation, particularly BIPV (Building-Integrated Photovoltaics), which harmonizes with architectural design.
Key Features
Design Integration
Achieves a refined and premium building appearance without compromising aesthetics
Space Efficiency
Utilizes the building envelope itself without requiring additional land
Energy Independence
Reduces electricity costs through self-consumption power generation
Realization of ESG Management
Enhances eco-friendly brand image while fulfilling corporate social responsibility
