Energy Self-Sufficiency Project

This project aims to enhance energy supply stability and strengthen the energy competitiveness of tenant companies by establishing a foundation for activating distributed energy resources within industrial complexes.

  • Applicant : Smart Green Industrial Complex
  • Establishment of distributed energy infrastructure and stabilization of energy supply within the industrial complex
  • Construction of a management system for distributed energy supply and demand management
  • Support for carbon neutrality and RE100 implementation for tenant companies
  • Installation of facilities related to profit-sharing for companies and workers within the industrial complex, as well as carbon reduction initiatives
Key Project Features
  • Activation of Distributed Energy
    Enhances supply stability by establishing distributed energy resources within industrial complexes
  • Energy Management System 구축
    Integrates and manages the entire process of energy production, storage, and consumption
  • Support for RE100 and Carbon Neutrality
    Supports 100% renewable energy transition and ESG initiatives for industrial complexes and enterprises
  • Profit Reinvestment
    Returning profits to companies and workers within the industrial complex (Solar Income)

Energy Self-Sufficiency Infrastructure Components

  • Renewable Energy Generation Facilities
    Electricity and heat are directly produced through solar power, wind power, fuel cells, and geothermal energy.
  • Energy Storage System (ESS)
    Stores surplus energy and discharges it when needed to ensure stable power supply.
  • Integrated Energy Management System (EMS)
    Monitors production, storage, and consumption in real time and optimizes overall energy flow.
  • Distributed Energy Network
    Connects multiple small-scale generators and ESS units to enhance regional energy self-sufficiency.
  • Smart Grid · Digital Infrastructure
    Applies IoT, big data, and AI technologies to enable predictive and intelligent energy operations.
  • RE100 · Carbon Neutrality Support Framework
    Provides the foundation for 100% renewable energy transition and carbon credit trading to support ESG management.

Benefits for Businesses

From reducing energy costs to achieving RE100 compliance and securing government incentives, this program delivers tangible advantages that strengthen corporate competitiveness.

  • Operating Cost Reduction & Revenue Generation
    Build a revenue model through self-consumption of generated power and surplus electricity sales.
  • RE100 & ESG Compliance
    Strengthen eco-friendly corporate image and respond to global supply chain requirements.
  • Government Support & Incentives
    Access policy benefits such as subsidies, tax incentives, and R&D grants.
  • Community Co-Prosperity
    Enhance regional energy self-sufficiency while creating social and environmental value.
