<p data-start="553" data-end="609"><strong data-start="553" data-end="609"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">Session 1: BIPV Technology for Zero-Energy Buildings</span></strong></p>
<p data-start="611" data-end="920"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">The seminar began with an introduction to BIPV (Building-Integrated Photovoltaics), a solar power system that integrates photovoltaic modules into building exterior materials such as walls, roofs, and windows.</span><br data-start="820" data-end="823"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">
This approach offers both aesthetic and functional advantages while maximizing energy efficiency.</span></p>
<p data-start="922" data-end="1151"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">With the growing enforcement of zero-energy building policies, BIPV is rapidly emerging as a key solution for eco-friendly construction. Hanbit Innotech is one of the leading companies providing optimized solutions in this field.</span></p>
<p data-start="1153" data-end="1411"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">The presentation covered real-world project cases, along with key technical considerations for BIPV during the architectural design phase, including economic feasibility and maintenance aspects. Future scalability and expansion potential were also discussed.</span></p><p data-start="1153" data-end="1411"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;"><br></span></p>
<hr data-start="1413" data-end="1416">
<p data-start="1418" data-end="1475"><strong data-start="1418" data-end="1475"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">Session 2: Introduction to Virtual Net Metering (VNM)</span></strong></p>
<p data-start="1477" data-end="1810"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">The seminar also introduced Virtual Net Metering (VNM), a technology that has recently gained significant attention.</span><br data-start="1593" data-end="1596"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">
VNM is a system that allows electricity generated from a single power facility to be distributed to multiple users who are not physically connected, with electricity charges settled based on individual consumption.</span></p>
<p data-start="1812" data-end="2060"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">This represents an evolution from the traditional single-user net metering model to a multi-user, shared energy model. It is particularly effective in promoting energy self-sufficiency in urban areas and enabling community-based energy transitions.</span></p>
<p data-start="2062" data-end="2248"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">Hanbit Innotech is currently developing a VNM system tailored to the domestic market, and is validating its practical applicability through pilot projects and real-world implementations.</span></p>
<p data-start="2250" data-end="2451"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">During the seminar, detailed explanations were provided on how the system operates, the regulatory framework required for implementation, and real-case demonstrations that highlighted its practicality.</span></p><p data-start="2250" data-end="2451"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;"><br></span></p>

<p data-start="2696" data-end="2947"><span style="font-family: 돋움, Dotum; font-size: 12pt;">In particular, VNM and BIPV are key technologies—each contributing to more flexible energy consumption structures and building-based energy self-sufficiency.
