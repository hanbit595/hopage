Is Our Village Eligible to Apply?

What is a Solar Income Village?
A resident-led solar power generation project that builds a local energy self-sufficiency foundation and revitalizes the regional economy through profit-sharing.

  • Eligible Villages
    • Construction and operation of village solar plants
    • Power sales profits used for community welfare
    • Resident income return and shared profit creation
    • Village assembly approval required
    • More than 70% resident consent required
  • Project Operator
    • Formation of a local-based (social) cooperative
    • Participation of at least 10 residents living over 1 year
    • Cooperative responsible for operation and management
    • Application process in cooperation with local government
    • Village assembly and board-led decision making
  • Solar Power Scale
    • Standard scale: 300kW ~ 1,000kW
    • Projects exceeding 1,000kW may be exceptionally selected

Key Benefits of a 1MW Solar Income Village Power Plant

From land area to profit optimization strategy

Scale
  • Required area based on 1MW generation capacity
  • Approx. 2,000 pyeong for rooftop type
  • Approx. 3,000~4,000 pyeong for ground/floating type
  • Approx. 4,000~5,000 pyeong for agrivoltaics
Annual Output
  • Estimated annual production: 1,314,000kWh
  • Based on 1,000kW × 3.6kWh × 365 days
  • Stable renewable energy generation
Energy Independence
  • Enough power for approx. 391 households
  • Based on annual consumption of a 4-person household
  • Building a local energy self-sufficiency system
Economic Benefits
  • REC weighting benefits through resident participation
  • Increased profitability and stable operation
  • Additional REC weight bonus of +0.2 available
