KEPCO Power Receiving Optimization

By removing high-voltage transformers within industrial sites (factories) and installing KEPCO's low-voltage transformers to receive direct low-voltage power,
electricity costs can be reduced by 25–35%.

High-Voltage Transformer
KEPCO Pad-Mounted Transformer
Application of a Lower Electricity Tariff Through Power Supply Method Conversion Work
  • Reduction in Electricity Costs
    Conversion of high-voltage receiving power to 380V / 220V three-phase low-voltage
    initial supply and optimized tariff adjustment,
    enabling up to 25–35% cost savings
  • Reduction in Maintenance Costs
    No need for in-house transformers,
    eliminating maintenance expenses
  • Enhanced Safety
    Supplied at low voltage,
    eliminating electric shock risks compared to high voltage
  • Wide Applicability
    Low-voltage supply available for capacities under 500kW
    and up to less than 1000kW
